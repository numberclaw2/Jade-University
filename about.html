About Jade University

A fast track, not a shortcut.

Jade University is the accelerated job-readiness companion to the broader Alfred University AU-ESET 301 curriculum.

What Jade changes

It changes the sequence and emphasis: DC fundamentals and component recognition, schematic/PCB/datasheet literacy, DMM/bench-supply/oscilloscope work, systematic troubleshooting/test procedures, and workmanship come first. Digital logic, protocols, and the capstone strengthen the path afterward.

What Jade does not change

It does not lower the instructional standard. The site keeps Alfred’s ground-up teaching principles, worked examples, contextual resources, glossary support, Learn/Study separation, deliberate completion, knowledge checks, practical evidence records, accessibility, and progress persistence.

Relationship to CETa

CETa-aligned concepts remain visible where relevant, but CETa is not treated as a gate that prevents job applications.

Relationship to Alfred

Alfred remains the broader long-form electronics-to-embedded learning system. Jade is the focused route for becoming employable sooner.
